## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the institutional prestige, discretion, and intellectual rigor required of an elite Brazilian legal counsel. The aesthetic merges traditional juristic gravitas with sharp, contemporary editorial clarity. It is designed to inspire profound confidence among institutional leaders, private wealth clients, and enterprise corporations facing high-stakes advisory and litigation matters.

The visual style is characterized by:
- **Architectural Precision:** Absolute square geometries (`roundedness: 0`), fine hairline structure, and deliberate, disciplined structural alignment reminiscent of monumental civic architecture.
- **Editorial Contrast:** High-contrast juxtapositions between classical serif display typography and functional, geometric sans-serif structural prose.
- **Subdued Luxury:** Generous expanses of warm alabaster and bone-cream negative space illuminated by calibrated brushed brass and deep, authoritative midnight navy.
- **Controlled Restraint:** Rejection of gratuitous ornamentation, heavy dropshadows, or rounded consumer trends in favor of timeless, razor-sharp institutional permanence.

## Colors

The color palette is built upon warm, tactile parchment tones, tempered by deeply saturated navy ink and refined brushed brass accents:

- **Primary Accent (`#C59B4B`):** Warm brushed brass. Reserved for critical focal points, active state indicators, key markers, and distinguished typographic touches.
- **Deep Navy (`#0C1A30` / `#14213D`):** Replaces pure black entirely. Used for dominant headings, primary action buttons, structural dividing frames, and high-priority legal iconography. It delivers high-contrast legibility without synthetic harshness.
- **Secondary Brass / Ochre (`#9A7B38`):** Deep burnished gold used for borders, hover states, sub-labels, and subtle linear highlights.
- **Surfaces & Grounds:**
  - Base Canvas: Warm Alabaster (`#FDFCF7`)
  - Elevated Container / Secondary Canvas: Soft Cream (`#F8F6F0`)
  - Recessed / Structural Fill: Bone Linen (`#F0EDE5`)
- **Border & Rule Accent (`#E5DFD5`):** Hairline delineation token ensuring balanced contrast across light surfaces without visual clutter.

## Typography

Typography establishes an authoritative rhythm between classic judicial scholarship and modern legal pragmatism:

- **Headlines & Titles (`Playfair Display`):** Deliberately chosen for its transitional, high-contrast letterforms. Headings should be set with close line heights and slight negative tracking in larger sizes to convey gravitas. Use italics sparingly for quotes, Latin maxims, or prominent jurisdictional citations.
- **Body & Continuous Reading (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Provides immaculate legibility across complex briefing clauses, contracts, and long-form legal articles.
- **Labels, Microcopy & Metadata (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Rendered in medium to bold weights with uppercase styling and positive tracking (`0.06em` to `0.1em`) to evoke legal document archiving and refined formal indexing.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors classical architectural proportion, utilizing a disciplined 12-column grid system anchored by unyielding structural gutters and broad frame margins:

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid with `3rem` section margins and `1.5rem` internal gutters. Sections are framed by horizontal 1px hairline rules (`#E5DFD5`) to enforce cadence.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column grid with `2rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters. High-density components (e.g., attorney rosters, transaction records) fold from 3 columns into 2 columns.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Multi-column editorial modules collapse into sequential vertical stacks.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Controlled strictly through multi-tier spacing increments. Editorial sections favor expansive vertical breathing room (`space-2xl` and `space-3xl`) to project an elite, unhurried institutional composure.

## Elevation & Depth

To sustain the architectural and print-inspired character, this system rejects soft volumetric dropshadows and blurred glass surfaces. Spatial hierarchy is articulated strictly through:

- **Tonal Stepping:** Layering is achieved by placing elevated content containers (`#FDFCF7`) over baseline structural grounds (`#F8F6F0` or `#F0EDE5`).
- **Hairline Framing:** Visual separation relies on crisp 1px borders colored in `#E5DFD5` or burnished gold `#C59B4B`. Overlapping panels use solid 1px borders rather than diffuse shadow drops.
- **Architectural Edge Shadows:** When physical floating separation is unavoidable (such as executive dropdown menus, legal search modals, or floating document drawers), use an ultra-crisp, low-bleed offset shadow tinted in navy:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 0 0 #0C1A30` (direct flat drop) or `box-shadow: 0 12px 24px -6px rgba(12, 26, 48, 0.08), 0 0 0 1px #E5DFD5`.

## Shapes

The shape philosophy strictly adheres to `roundedness: 0`.

- **Corner Radius:** Every corner across buttons, cards, dialogs, form inputs, chips, and navigational headers is precisely 90 degrees (`border-radius: 0px`).
- **Rationale:** Strict geometric corners evoke legal parchment, stone tablets, classical porticos, and formal binding. This eliminates casual or playful motifs, reinforcing sovereign authority and uncompromising discipline.

## Components

### Buttons & Actions
- **Primary:** Background in `#0C1A30` (Navy), text in `#FDFCF7`, 0px border radius, with padding of `0.875rem 2rem`. On hover, background shifts to `#14213D` with a sharp 1px bottom accent border in `#C59B4B`.
- **Secondary (Brass Accent):** Background in transparent fill, 1px solid border in `#C59B4B`, text in `#0C1A30` with tracking. On hover, fills solid `#C59B4B` with text in `#FDFCF7`.
- **Tertiary / Link:** Plain text in `#0C1A30` with an unbroken 1px underline in `#C59B4B` offset by 4px.

### Cards & Editorial Containers
- Constructed on `#FFFFFF` or `#FDFCF7` with a 1px solid perimeter border in `#E5DFD5`.
- Zero box-shadow in default state. Hover interactions feature a subtle transition of the top border from `#E5DFD5` to a 2px brass border `#C59B4B`, indicating interactive focus.

### Input Fields & Selects
- 0px border radius with a solid `#FDFCF7` ground. Framed by a 1px border in `#E5DFD5`.
- Active/Focus state replaces the perimeter border with `#0C1A30` and provides a hairline bottom edge in `#C59B4B`. Labels sit uppercase in `label-sm` above the field with generous vertical clearance.

### Chips & Badges
- Strict rectangular tags (`border-radius: 0px`).
- Background `#F0EDE5` with text in `#0C1A30`, framed in `#E5DFD5`. Status or high-distinction badges use `#0C1A30` background with gold `#C59B4B` typography.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Sharp 16px square containers (`border-radius: 0px`).
- Inactive state: 1px border in `#0C1A30`.
- Checked state: Filled with `#0C1A30` carrying an exact brass checkmark (`#C59B4B`) or centered geometric square inner pip.

### Lists & Legal Case Dossiers
- Clean tabular lists bounded by top and bottom 1px borders in `#E5DFD5`.
- List rows feature generous vertical padding (`space-md` to `space-lg`) with alternating metadata badges, practicing partner signatures, and publication dates set in uppercase label styling.
